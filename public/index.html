What Is Liquidity?

Liquidity means clusters of resting orders waiting to be filled. Where lots of orders sit = high liquidity zone. Where few sit = low liquidity zone — like a busy market stall versus a quiet back alley.

Price $84,500 ──── SELL STOPS / RESISTANCE ──── (liquidity resting above) ↑ ↑ ↑ ↑ ↑ ↑ ↑ ↑ ↑ ↑ $84,000 ──────────────────────────────── $83,500 ──── current price area ───────── $83,000 ──────────────────────────────── ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ $82,500 ──── BUY STOPS / SUPPORT ──────── (liquidity resting below)

Sweep of Liquidity Above/Below a POI

POI = Point of Interest, a key level where significant orders sit. A sweep is when price briefly spikes past that level, triggers the resting stops, then reverses.

$85,200 ─── SWEEP (spike above, grabs stops) ← smart money sells here ↗ ↘ $85,000 ─── OLD HIGH (POI) ─────────────────── stops sitting here $84,800 ────────────────────────────────────── gets triggered $84,500 ────────────────────────────────────── price reverses $84,000 ──── TRUE DIRECTION IS DOWN ───────── $83,500 ↓ ↓ ↓ move begins here after sweep

How To Trade A Liquidity Sweep

Wait for the sweep to complete — don't chase the spike. Entry: when price closes back below the swept level. Stop loss: above the sweep high. Target: next major support level below.

Market Structure

The pattern of higher highs/lows or lower highs/lows tells you whether the market is bullish, bearish, or transitioning.

TermMeaningSignal
BOS — Break of StructurePrice breaks a prior significant high or lowTrend continuing
CHoCH — Change of CharacterPrice breaks structure in the opposite directionTrend reversing

Type 1 — Trend Continuation

Clean trending structure: consistent higher highs + higher lows (up), or lower highs + lower lows (down). Trade the pullbacks/bounces back into structure.

Type 2 — Trend Reversal

Begins with a CHoCH — the first break of structure in the opposite direction. Highest-probability entry point: catching a reversal early.

ScenarioTypeEntryRisk
Strong trending marketType 1Buy/sell pullbacksLower
Trend just reversedType 2Enter on CHoCH breakMedium
Choppy sideways marketNeitherStay outHigh
Inverted Head and Shoulders

A reliable reversal pattern forming at the bottom of a downtrend, signalling a reversal to the upside.

PartWhat It IsSignificance
Left ShoulderFirst low — price drops and bouncesDowntrend still in control
HeadSecond low — deeper than left shoulderFinal push of sellers
Right ShoulderThird low — higher than the headSellers losing power
NecklineLine connecting the two bounce highsBreaking above = entry signal

How To Trade It

Identify the three lows, draw the neckline, wait — don't enter early. Enter when price closes above the neckline on good volume. Stop loss below the right shoulder. Target = neckline + head height.

✅ Must form after a clear downtrend · ✅ Head lower than both shoulders · ✅ Shoulders roughly equal height · ✅ Volume rises on breakout · ✅ Wait for a candle close above the neckline · ❌ Never enter mid-pattern
